## Activity

Debate using the rough structure below debate.
Stress importance of maintaining composure.
No one here is going to change the world with their arguments this is just a fun exercise.
Each student will get 5pts for participating in the debate.

Debate starters:

* Is it ok for AI company's to train AI on public posts? Just because something is public does it mean it's free for
  anyone to do what ever they want to it?
* Does AI in education reduce learning. Do people always use AI to avoid critical thinking or is it a tool for learning.
* AI currently uses a lot of electricity. Is the use of electricity justified. If not who is responsible for its use.
* Since AI was trained on all of our data. Who should "OWN" AI?
* Will the current way that AI works (GEN AI) ever become AGI?
